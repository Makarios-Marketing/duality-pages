Find your curl pattern

Every curl is cut a little differently

Waves, curls, and coils each spring and shrink in their own way. Tap your pattern to see what we focus on when we cut it.

Wavy · pattern

Waves that hold their shape

Waves can fall flat at the root and puff up in humidity. We cut to keep weight where it helps your wave form, and take out bulk without losing the shape.

Spirals with room to spring

Curls shrink as they dry, sometimes a lot. We cut with that spring in mind, so the length you see once your curls dry is the length you actually wanted.

Coils, balanced all around

Tight coils need shape and moisture. We cut and shape so your coils sit full and even all the way around, never heavy on the bottom or triangular.

Not sure which you are? Most heads have more than one pattern. We will read yours at your appointment.

Good to know

Curly cut questions

How much is a curly haircut in Williamsville?

A curly cut with styling starts at $75, and a curly style on its own starts at $50. Prices are “and up,” depending on your length, density, and how much time your curls need. Tell us about your hair when you book and we will give you a clear idea before we start.

How should I come to my curly cut appointment?

If you can, come with your curls styled the way you normally wear them, so we can see your pattern, your shrinkage, and where your curls want to sit before we cut.

What is the difference between a curly cut and a regular haircut?

A regular haircut is usually planned around straight, wet lengths. A curly cut is planned around how your curls actually behave, including shrinkage, volume, and how each section springs, so the shape works when your hair dries naturally.

Do you cut wavy and coily hair too?

Yes. We cut all textures, from loose waves to tight coils. Every curl pattern needs a slightly different approach, and we will tailor the cut to yours.

How often should I get a curly haircut?

Many clients come in every 8 to 12 weeks, but it depends on your curls and the shape you want to keep. We will suggest a timeline at your appointment.

Will you show me how to style my curls at home?

Yes. We finish every curly cut with a style and walk you through the products and steps we used, so you can recreate your curls at home.
