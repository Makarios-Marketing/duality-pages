Good to Know

Questions before you apply

Do I need to bring my own clients?
No. We help you grow a loyal book in a salon that people in Williamsville already know and trust. Walk-ins and referrals come through the door, and we support you in building from there.
Is this booth rental or a team salon?
A team salon. We are looking for stylists who want to grow with us, not just rent a chair. You are part of the team, with real support behind you.
Do you hire newly licensed or soon-to-be-licensed stylists?
Yes. If you are a licensed cosmetologist in New York State, or about to be, we would love to meet you. Mentorship from our senior stylists is part of how we work here.
What is the schedule like?
We are open Monday through Saturday by appointment, and we protect your schedule rather than overbooking it, so you have the time to do your best work on every client.
What happens after I apply?
We reach out to set up a time to meet, show you around, and talk through the chair. If it feels like a fit on both sides, we go from there.
