Keep it bright

Making vivids last in Western New York

Vivid color is the first thing to fade, and a Buffalo year is hard on it. Here is what we tell every vivid client.

Wash cool

Hot water opens the hair and lets color slip out. Rinse as cool as you can stand it.

Beat the hard water

Buffalo’s hard water leaves mineral buildup that dulls vivid shades fast. We will suggest products that help.

Guard against summer

Sun, lake days, and pool chlorine fade bright color. Cover up or rinse right after swimming.

Refresh on schedule

A quick color refresh between visits keeps your shade looking like day one instead of week six.

Good to know

Vivid color questions

How much does vivid or fashion color cost in Williamsville?

Vivid and fashion color is priced at your consultation, which is free. The price depends on your starting color, how much lightening your hair needs first, and how much of your hair gets color, so we would rather quote you an honest number after we see your hair than guess.

Do I need to bleach my hair first?

Most of the time, yes. Vivid shades show up brightest on hair that has been lightened first. How much depends on your current color and the shade you want, and we will lighten only as far as your hair can safely handle.

How long does vivid hair color last?

Vivid shades fade faster than natural tones, usually over a few weeks, and some colors fade faster than others. Cool water, color-safe shampoo, and washing less often help a lot, and we will send you home with a routine for your shade.

Can I try a vivid without going all in?

Absolutely. A money piece, peekaboo panels, or dipped ends are great ways to try a vivid with less commitment and easier upkeep. We can talk through the options at your consultation.

Can you do vivid color on dark hair?

Yes, with the right plan. Dark hair usually needs lightening first, and very dark hair may take more than one visit to get to a bright vivid safely. We will be honest about what is possible in one appointment.

What if I already have color in my hair?

Bring it in. Existing color, especially box dye, can change how your hair lightens, so we will look at your color history at your free consultation and plan from there.
