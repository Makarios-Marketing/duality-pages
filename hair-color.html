The approach

Color built around your hair, not a swatch book

Before any color goes on, we talk through your hair's history, the tone that works with your skin, and how often you really want to be back in the chair. A color that looks perfect the day you leave but is impossible to keep up is the wrong color.

So we build your color to flatter you and grow out softly, protect the condition of your hair while we do it, and finish with a gloss so you leave shiny and even. That is the difference between color you love for a week and color that still looks good as it grows.

Western New York

Color that stays rich through a Buffalo winter

Color has to survive the season here. Western New York's hard water and the dry indoor heat from November on pull the shine and tone out of color fast, leaving blondes brassy and darker shades dull. In summer, sun and the humidity off the lake do their own fading. We formula and gloss your color with all of it in mind, and send you home knowing how to protect it, so your color still looks rich in real Williamsville weather, not just the day you leave the chair.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much is hair color in Williamsville?

Color is priced to the service and the length and thickness of your hair, so the honest answer is that it depends. A root retouch starts around $62, while balayage, highlights, and all-over color are quoted at your consultation. Tell us your goal when you book and we will give you a clear estimate.

Do you do balayage and highlights?

Yes. Our Williamsville stylists do balayage, foilyage, highlights, and lowlights, from a few face-framing pieces to a full head. We will help you pick the technique that fits the look and the upkeep you want.

Can you cover gray?

Absolutely. We blend or fully cover gray and refresh your shade with a root retouch, starting around $62, timed to how quickly your roots show. We can go full coverage or a softer, lower-maintenance blend.

Do you do vivid or fashion colors?

We do. One of our stylists loves creative, vivid color, so pinks, coppers, and bold fashion shades are all on the table. Vivids usually need a consultation first so we can plan any lightening your hair needs.

Will coloring damage my hair?

We color with your hair's health in mind, using bond care and gentle formulas and never lifting further than your hair can handle in one visit. At your consultation we will tell you honestly what is realistic for your hair.

How do I keep my color looking fresh?

Color-safe products, cooler water, and a gloss between appointments go a long way, and they matter even more with Western New York's hard water. We will send you home with a simple routine to keep your Williamsville color rich between visits.
