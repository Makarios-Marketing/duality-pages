The approach

Highlights placed for you, not a pattern

Great highlights come down to where every foil goes. Before the first one goes in, your stylist makes three decisions with you, so the brightness lands where it flatters you most.

  1. i.

    Placement

    We look at your cut, where you part your hair, and whether you wear it up or down, then plan where each foil goes.

  2. ii.

    Weave

    Fine, blended pieces for a soft, natural lift, or bolder sections for more contrast. You choose the look.

  3. iii.

    Tone

    We finish with a toner matched to your skin, so your color rinses out bright and polished, never brassy or stripy.

Frequently asked questions

How much are highlights in Williamsville?

Partial highlights start at $95 and full highlights start at $130. Like all of our color, highlights are priced to the length and thickness of your hair, so prices are “and up.” Your color consultation is free, and we will give you a clear estimate before we start.

What is the difference between partial and full highlights?

Partial highlights are placed around your face and through the top layer, where brightness shows the most. Full highlights are placed all through your hair, including underneath, for bright, even dimension from root to end. If you are not sure which you need, we will help you choose at your consultation.

How often should I get my highlights redone?

Most clients come back every 6 to 10 weeks for foil highlights, depending on how much contrast there is between your highlights and your natural color. A toner refresh in between (from $40) keeps the color bright and knocks out brassiness.

Should I get highlights or balayage?

Choose foil highlights if you want brighter, more even lightness that starts closer to the root. Choose balayage (from $140) if you want a softer, hand-painted look that grows out without a line. Bring your inspiration photos and we will tell you which technique gets you there.

Can you add lowlights too?

Yes. Our Williamsville stylists do highlights and lowlights, and adding a few deeper pieces is a great way to bring back depth, blend in grays, or keep highlighted hair from looking flat. We will talk through it at your consultation.

Will highlights damage my hair?

We lighten with your hair’s health in mind, using bond care and never pushing further than your hair can handle in one sitting. The goal is bright highlights on hair that still feels soft and healthy.
