The approach

A haircut shaped around your hair, not a template

Before any scissors come out, we talk through your hair type, your face shape, and how much time you actually want to spend styling it in the morning. A cut that looks great in the chair but needs twenty minutes of work at home is the wrong cut.

So we shape your cut to work with your natural texture and grow out cleanly, then finish it with a shampoo and blowdry style so you leave knowing exactly how it is meant to sit. That is the difference between a haircut you like for a week and one that still looks right a month later.

Haircuts that hold up through a Buffalo winter

A cut has to survive the season here. From November on you are in and out of hats and hoods, and the dry indoor heat leaves ends rough and flat, so a shape that grows out gracefully matters more than a style that only looks right the day you leave. In the humid stretch of a Buffalo summer, the same thinking applies the other way, with cuts that still behave when the air is heavy off the lake. We cut with all of it in mind, so your haircut works in real Williamsville weather, not just in the chair.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much is a haircut in Williamsville?

Haircuts at our Williamsville salon start at $26, and every cut includes a shampoo and a blowdry style. Women's, men's, kids', and short clipper cuts are all priced based on the time and work involved, so the easiest way to get an exact number is to tell us what you are after when you book.

Do you cut men's and kids' hair, or just women's?

All of it. Our Williamsville stylists do women's haircuts, men's haircuts, and kids' cuts, which makes it easy for a whole family to book on the same visit. First haircuts for little ones are welcome too.

Do you do fades and short clipper cuts?

Yes. We do clipper cuts, tapers, and fades for anyone in Williamsville who likes their hair short and sharp, finished barber-style so it is easy to keep looking clean between visits.

How often should I get a haircut?

It depends on your style. Shorter cuts and fades usually look best with a trim every few weeks, while longer women's haircuts can stretch longer between visits. At your Williamsville haircut we will suggest a timeline that fits the shape you are wearing.

Do I need an appointment for a haircut?

We recommend booking ahead so you get the stylist and time you want. You can book your Williamsville haircut online, or call or text us at 716-998-8528 and we will find a spot that works.

How do I explain the haircut I want?

Bring a photo or two if you have them, and we will take it from there. A quick conversation at the start of your Williamsville haircut, about what you like and what you do not, tells us more than any single word for a style ever could.
