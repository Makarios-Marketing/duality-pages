The approach

Balayage that looks lived-in, not done

Good balayage is all about placement. Before we pick up a brush, we look at how your hair falls, where the light hits it, and how bright you want to go, then paint the lightness exactly where it will look natural.

Because it is painted on by hand instead of placed in a strict foil pattern, it melts into your own color at the root. We finish with a toner so the tone is soft and flattering, not brassy, and the color grows out gently so you are not racing back for a touch-up.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much is balayage in Buffalo and Williamsville?

Balayage at Duality starts at $140. Like all of our color, it is priced to the length and thickness of your hair, so prices are “and up.” Your color consultation is free, and we will give you a clear estimate before we start.

What is the difference between balayage and highlights?

Balayage is painted on by hand for a soft, blended result that grows out without a hard line. Foil highlights are placed in a more even pattern for brighter, more uniform lightness. Partial highlights start at $95 and full highlights at $130, and we will help you pick the right one for the look you want.

How often will I need to come back after balayage?

Because balayage grows out softly, most clients can go longer between visits than they would with foil highlights. A toner refresh in between (from $40) keeps the color bright and keeps brassiness away, and we will suggest a timeline that fits your hair.

Can I get balayage if I have dark hair?

Yes. Balayage works on dark hair too. We lighten gradually and only as far as your hair can handle in one visit, so a big change from very dark hair may take more than one appointment. We will be honest about that at your consultation.

Will balayage damage my hair?

We lighten with your hair’s health in mind, using bond care and never pushing further than your hair can handle in one sitting. The goal is bright, soft balayage on hair that still feels healthy.

How do I keep my balayage from turning brassy?

Color-safe products, cooler water, and a toner refresh between visits go a long way, and they matter even more with Buffalo’s hard water and summer sun. We will send you home with a simple routine to keep your balayage soft and bright.
